VOICE (copy the patterns out of your own sent mail, not from an idea of good writing):
- Opens "Hi [First]!" with the exclamation mark.
- "My name is [Name], I'm a [year] [subject] student at [University]."
- Uses "would love to" for asks.
- Sentences run a little long and comma-spliced. Do NOT polish that out.
- NEVER use em dashes. Never "I hope this finds you well", never "reach out",
  never "excited about the intersection of". No apologies, no "I am writing to".
